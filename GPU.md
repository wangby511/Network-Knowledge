## CPU & GPU

Difference:

CPUs have fewer cores (typically 4–64) optimized for complex, serial, or varied, tasks. GPUs have thousands of smaller, specialized cores built for high-throughput, parallel operations.

Processing Approach: CPUs handle tasks in series (one after another) and are designed to minimize latency for individual tasks. GPUs process massive amounts of data in parallel (thousands of tasks simultaneously) to maximize throughput.

While CPUs are essential for a computer to function, modern systems use GPUs to accelerate data-intensive, parallel tasks.
